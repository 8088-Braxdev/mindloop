Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

MindLoop is a nightly review app. You write what went well, what did not, and your three priorities for tomorrow, then you answer honestly about what you did. This page explains what data MindLoop handles and why. MindLoop is run by BraxCode Digitals Foundation. You can reach us at braxtonbruzzzy@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account details from Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile picture and an account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • What you write in MindLoop: your wins, challenges, main reason, lessons, your priorities with start times, and the moments you tap Start, Yes or No on a priority.
  • Technical data: like your IP address and browser type, which our hosting and login providers process to deliver the service and keep it secure.

MindLoop does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your reviews in your account.
  • To calculate your streak, weekly stats and charts.
  • To write an AI insight, only when you tap the button for it.

AI insights

When you request an insight, the reviews and calculated stats for that period are sent to GROQ to generate the text. Nothing is sent to it at any other time. The provider processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy. Because AI can make mistakes, treat insights as suggestions.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Google: sign-in.
  • Vercel: hosting.
  • GROQ: insight generation, as described above.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. These providers may store data on servers outside your country.

Copy on your device

To work offline, MindLoop keeps a copy of your reviews in your browser while you are signed in, plus any changes not yet saved to your account. The copy is removed when you sign out.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your reviews stay in your account until you delete them.
  • "Delete all data" permanently removes all your reviews.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time. This permanently deletes your reviews and saved insights. We keep only anonymous usage counts (no text, and nothing that identifies you) so our daily limits stay accurate.

Your choices

You can see, edit and delete what you have written. If you want something corrected or removed that you cannot change yourself, email us.

Security

Each account can only read and change its own reviews. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service is completely secure.

Children

MindLoop is not meant for children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you on the sign-in screen.

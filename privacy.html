Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

MindLoop is a nightly review app. You write what went well, what did not, and your three priorities for tomorrow, then you answer honestly about what you did. This page explains what data MindLoop handles and why. MindLoop is run by BraxCode Digitals Foundation. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +255 618 811 359 or by email at braxtonbruzzzy@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account details from Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile picture and an account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • What you write in MindLoop: your wins, challenges, main reason, lessons, your priorities with the start and finish times you set, and the moments you tap Start, Yes or No on a priority.
  • Technical data: like your IP address and browser type, which our hosting and login providers process to deliver the service and keep it secure.

MindLoop does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your reviews in your account.
  • To calculate your streak, weekly stats and charts.
  • To write an AI insight, only when you tap the button for it.

AI insights

When you request an insight, the reviews and calculated stats for that period are sent to GROQ to generate the text. That insight is written in the language you wrote those reviews in (Swahili or English). Nothing is sent to it at any other time. The provider processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy. Because AI can make mistakes, treat insights as suggestions.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Google: sign-in.
  • Vercel: hosting.
  • GROQ: insight generation, as described above.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. These providers may store data on servers outside your country.

Copy on your device

To work offline, MindLoop keeps a copy of your reviews in your browser while you are signed in, plus any changes not yet saved to your account. The copy is removed when you sign out.

Reminders and notifications

You can ask MindLoop to remind you before a priority starts, using the start time you set. Reminders appear inside the app, and — if you allow notifications for this site — also in your phone's notification tray, including while the app is closed. To do that while closed, your device creates a Web Push subscription and MindLoop stores it on your account: the subscription's address at your browser's push service, its two encryption keys, your time zone and your quiet hours. Nothing from your reviews is stored with it. The reminder message is a short nudge built from the priority's own text and start time, and it is delivered by your browser's push service, which necessarily sees the device address and the encrypted message.

MindLoop asks your browser for notification permission when you finish the tour on your first visit, and Account always has an "Allow notifications" control, so the ask is never hidden away. Your browser decides whether to show its own prompt, and MindLoop never turns notifications on without that answer. You can say no, and you can change the decision at any time in your browser's site settings. Account tells you plainly whether closed-app reminders are working on the device you are using. Refusing does not stop the reminder card from appearing inside the app.

Because the server sends closed-app reminders, it keeps a small log of them: your account ID, which priority the reminder was for (its position in that day's plan) and the date. The log contains none of your text. It exists so the same reminder is never sent twice, and old rows are deleted automatically. Signing out on this device, deleting that device's subscription, blocking notifications, or deleting your account removes the stored subscription.

You can also set quiet hours in Account. During those hours MindLoop sends no notification to that device, including closed-app reminders, while the reminder card still appears inside the app. Quiet hours are kept on your device, and a copy is stored with that device's push subscription so the server does not send a reminder during them. They only silence MindLoop: they do not change your phone's Do Not Disturb setting and do not mute other apps.

A "Done means…" line, a completion note and the reason you pick when moving a priority are part of your review: they are saved to your account like the rest of it, and they are only sent to the AI provider when you ask for an AI insight, as described above.

Feedback and ratings

After you have used MindLoop for a while, it may ask once for a star rating and an optional note. Your answer is kept on your device. If you tap Send, your rating and note open in WhatsApp so you can choose to send them to us yourself. Nothing is sent unless you do that.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your reviews stay in your account until you delete them.
  • Saved AI insights and a usage log: each insight you request is saved to your account so you can read it again. We also log how many insight requests were made and how large they were (counts only, no text) to enforce daily limits.
  • "Delete all data" permanently removes all your reviews and saved insights.
  • "Delete this insight" removes a saved insight. That period will not get a new one.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time. This permanently deletes your reviews and saved insights. We keep only anonymous usage counts (no text, and nothing linked to you) so our daily limits stay accurate.

    Security

    Each account can only read and change its own reviews. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service is completely secure.
